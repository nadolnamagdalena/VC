One Patomy case

Rules ∙ Evidence ∙ Gaps & questions ∙ Decisions

01 · Mandate intelligence

Eligibility you can prove

Test every application against your fund's formal rules: state aid, SME status, fund limits, region and instrument, with every finding linked to the page or register it came from.

  • Red only with proof. Missing information is never a fail, it stays Orange until resolved.
  • Judgement stays human. Calls like the SME group test go to the manager, not the model.
  • Every fund checked separately. Patomy suggests the best-fitting route.

02 · Case continuity

One case, from application to investment committee.

Formal criteria, missing information, due diligence questions, evidence and decisions stay connected in one case. Fewer manual handovers, and an investment case that is easier to assess and to defend.

  • Gaps become questions. Missing facts turn into founder requests and due diligence items.
  • Nothing gets lost. Open issues follow the case all the way to the committee.
  • Hand over with context. The next person picks up the full case, not a forwarded deck.

03 · Institutional memory

Context for better decisions

Patomy remembers what your team knew when it decided, who decided, and why. Your fund's reasoning stops living in inboxes and in the heads of people who leave.

  • A snapshot of every decision. The evidence, rules and gaps as they stood on the day.
  • Overrides on the record. Every human change is logged with its reason.
  • Learn from outcomes. See how approved and declined cases developed afterwards.

04 · Built into your stack

Works where your team already works.

Patomy connects to the systems your fund already runs. No migration, no new place to keep records.

  • Your CRM stays the system of record. Patomy adds the evidence and the decision.
  • Known companies and founders recognised. Matched against your CRM and the KvK register.
  • Bring your own data licences. Connect the data providers you already pay for.

How it works

AI reads. Rules decide. People own the decision.

AI only reads documents and points to where it found each fact. Fixed rules, configured to your fund, decide every status and route.

  1. 01 / Intake

    Collect the application

    Pitch deck, founder form, annual accounts and cash forecast, matched to the KvK register.

  2. 02 / Check

    Test it against your rules

    Red, Orange or Green per criterion and per fund, each linked to its source.

  3. 03 / Clarify

    Close the gaps

    Missing facts become questions for the founder and due diligence items for the team.

  4. 04 / Decide

    Route, decide, remember

    The case reaches the right team and the committee with its full history on record.
